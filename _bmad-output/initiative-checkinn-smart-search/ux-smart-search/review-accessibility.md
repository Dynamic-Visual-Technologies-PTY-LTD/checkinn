# Accessibility review: CheckInn Smart search UX delta

Reviewer stance: adversarial, WCAG 2.1 AA plus practical screen-reader, keyboard, touch, speech-input, low-vision and projector use. Scope: the delta `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md` in this folder, and the four key-screen mocks in `.working/` (`key-home-smart.html`, `key-results-thandi.html`, `key-results-weak.html`, `key-hotel-bestfit.html`), read as HTML and CSS. Inherited rules: the root `DESIGN.md` and the MVP `EXPERIENCE.md` Accessibility Floor. Contrast ratios are computed with WCAG relative luminance from the token hex values. The mocks illustrate the spine and the spine is the contract, so a mock-only defect is reported only where a build could copy it or where it shows a gap in the spine.

## Verdict

Close, but not ready to build against as written. Every new colour pair passes, and the gradient border passes 3:1 along its whole length. The live-region, focus and label rules are mostly right in intent. The gaps are in the details a builder will copy: the Criteria chip names fail Label in Name, the chips cannot reflow under enlarged text, and the notices sit before the heading that is meant to carry them, with a broken `aria-describedby` in the mock. Most fixes are one-line spine changes.

## Findings

- **[high]** Criteria chip accessible names fail Label in Name (2.5.3, Level A). The visible text is "Max R 2 000 per night" and the spine prescribes the name "Remove Max 2 000 rand per night" (EXPERIENCE Voice and Tone "Formats", Component Patterns "Criteria chip"; mock `aria-label="Remove Max 2 000 rand per night"` in `key-results-thandi.html`). The visible string is not contained in the name: "R" is dropped and "rand" is inserted mid-phrase. A speech-input user who says "click Max R 2 000 per night" may get no match, and the same applies to every price chip. *Fix:* do not override the name with `aria-label`. Use the visible text plus a visually hidden prefix, `<button><span class="sr-only">Remove </span>Max R 2 000 per night<svg aria-hidden="true">…</svg></button>`, so the name is "Remove Max R 2 000 per night". If the spoken form "rand" is wanted, add it after the visible string ("Remove Max R 2 000 per night, 2 000 rand"), never in place of it. Amend the "Formats" line so the MVP spoken-price rule does not apply to control names.

- **[high]** Chips cannot reflow under enlarged text or text spacing (1.4.4, 1.4.10, 1.4.12). `.crit` and the root `.chip` are `white-space: nowrap`, and the price copy is joined with non-breaking spaces by design (DESIGN Components "Criteria chip", "Best fit chip"; mock `.crit{…white-space:nowrap}`). At default text on a 320px viewport "MAX R 2 000 PER NIGHT" is about 250px wide and fits the 290px content box. At 200% text on a phone it is about 440px against 290–345px, so the row scrolls sideways or the chip is clipped, which also breaks the spine's own ban on horizontally scrolling chip rows. "BEST FIT FOR YOUR SEARCH" on the Hotel page room card does the same. Letter-spacing at 0.12em (1.4.12) widens both further. *Fix:* keep the non-breaking space only between "R" and the amount ("R 2 000"), allow the chip label to wrap (`white-space: normal`, `max-width: 100%`, `min-width: 0`, `text-align: start`), and keep the × pinned at the end with `flex: none`. Size chip text in `rem` so text resizing applies. Add "chips wrap inside themselves at 200% text on a 320px viewport" to the Accessibility Floor.

- **[high]** Notices sit before the heading that is supposed to carry them, and the mock's link is broken (1.3.1, 1.3.2). The spine puts notices "between the chips and the headline" and references them from the headline with `aria-describedby` (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Notice (Smart search)", Accessibility Floor "Headline after navigation"). Descriptions are read only when the heading receives focus, and inconsistently: NVDA and JAWS read them on focus, VoiceOver often only after a pause or not at all, and no screen reader reads them when a guest arrives by heading navigation (H) or reads on from the heading in browse mode, because the notice is above it. The fallback notice ("these results are in the usual order") changes how the whole list should be read, so missing it matters. In `key-results-weak.html` the desktop heading has `aria-describedby="n1"` but no element has `id="n1"`, and the phone variant has no `aria-describedby` at all. *Fix:* put the notices in the DOM directly after the heading (visually they can stay above it with CSS `order` inside a flex column, or simply move them under the heading, which also reads well). Keep `aria-describedby` on the heading pointing at every shown notice's `id`, in priority order (`aria-describedby="notice-fallback notice-place"`). Add a component test that asserts the description text, so a dangling id fails CI.

- **[medium]** The Smart search box element is unspecified, and the mock models it as a non-editable `span` (4.1.2, 1.3.1, 3.3.2). The spine says "a real `label`" and "one line that grows to two … Enter submits and never adds a new line", but never names the element (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Smart search box"). The mock uses `<span role="textbox" aria-multiline="true" aria-labelledby>` with `aria-readonly`, which is not focusable, not editable and has no `maxlength`; a build that copies it, or uses `contenteditable`, loses native editing, IME, autocorrect, `readonly` and the form submit. A multi-line textbox where Enter submits also contradicts what screen readers announce ("multi-line edit"). *Fix:* specify a native `<textarea rows="1" maxlength="300" enterkeyhint="search">` with `<label for>` "Describe your stay", grown to two lines with `field-sizing: content` or a small resize script, and `readonly` (the attribute, not `aria-readonly`) while submitting. Handle Enter on `keydown` (submit, `preventDefault`), and ignore it during IME composition (`event.isComposing`). Add a visually hidden description, "Press Enter to search", to the field's `aria-describedby`, so the Enter behaviour is not a surprise in a multi-line field. Do not set `aria-multiline="false"` on a textarea.

- **[medium]** The "Reading your search…" live region is created already populated, so it will often not be announced (4.1.3). In `key-home-smart.html` the submitting state renders `<p role="status" aria-live="polite">Reading your search…</p>` as a new node with its text. Screen readers announce changes to a live region that already exists, not one inserted with content. The same applies to the "Match reasons added" region and the chip-removal count (EXPERIENCE Accessibility Floor "Status while submitting", "Match reasons"; Component Patterns "Criteria chip"). The mock also swaps the field's `aria-describedby` from the counter to the status, so the counter description is lost while submitting. *Fix:* render each live region empty at page load and set its text on the event. Clear "Reading your search…" on navigation. Keep the field described by both, `aria-describedby="ss-hint ss-counter ss-status"`, with ids that always exist. Do not fire "Match reasons added" if the guest has already left the page.

- **[medium]** While a Match reason loads, its label is exposed but its content is hidden, so the card reads "Why it matches" followed by nothing (1.3.1). The spine says the card reads "as if the reason were absent until it arrives" (EXPERIENCE Accessibility Floor "Skeleton"), but the mock hides only the skeleton (`<p class="mr-l">Why it matches</p><div class="mr-sk" aria-hidden="true">`). In forced-colours mode the skeleton's background is also removed, so sighted users see the same orphan label. *Fix:* put `aria-hidden="true"` on the whole Match reason block while it loads and remove it when the sentence arrives. Mark the label and sentence as a pair (`<p><span class="label">Why it matches</span> <span>…</span></p>`, or a `dl`), so the label is read with its sentence. In forced colours give the skeleton a 1px `CanvasText` outline, or accept it as invisible since the block is hidden from AT anyway.

- **[medium]** The Best-fit pre-selection is not conveyed where a non-visual user operates it (1.3.1, 3.3.2). The chip is a sibling of the Room type heading, and the select is named "Family Room rooms" (`key-hotel-bestfit.html`; EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Best fit chip", "Room table (changed)"). A screen-reader user tabbing to the select hears "Family Room rooms, 1" with no hint that CheckInn chose it, and focus lands on the hotel name far above, so the pre-filled booking summary and enabled Reserve are not explained before a guest can reserve. In the desktop table the room-name cell is a `td`, not the row header the MVP review asked for, so the chip is not part of the row's context either. *Fix:* make the room-name cell `<th scope="row">` with the heading and the chip inside it. Give the select `aria-describedby` pointing at the chip's `id` ("Best fit for your search"). Add one line to the booking summary while the pre-selection is untouched, for example "Family Room picked for your search. Change it in the table.", in `body-sm` muted, so the choice is explained in reading order and on the phone bottom bar's full summary.

- **[medium]** The Match reason and Best-fit line are described as "inside" the card link, and the mocks use a whole-card `<a>` (1.3.1, 2.4.4). EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Listing card (changed)" says "The card stays one link named after the hotel; the Match reason and Best-fit room line are plain text inside it." The shipped card (`src/components/listing-card.tsx`) makes the hotel name the only link, stretched over the card, which is right. If "inside it" is built literally, either the link's name becomes the whole card text including the streamed reason, or an `aria-label` hides the reason from VoiceOver and TalkBack swipe navigation, which is the feature's main content. *Fix:* reword to "plain text inside the card, outside the hotel-name link (the MVP stretched link)". Keep "See availability" hidden from AT as in the MVP. The score breakdown table must also sit outside the link.

- **[medium]** On a 320px phone at 200% text the Smart search box shows about a dozen characters of a 300-character Query (1.4.4, 1.4.10). The row holds the 20px icon, the text area, two 44px icon buttons, three 8px gaps, 19px of left and right padding and the 2px border, which leaves roughly 135px for text at 320px. At 32px text (200% of `input-mobile`) and a two-line cap (`max-height: 3em`), the guest sees about two short lines and must scroll inside a tiny box to check what they typed (DESIGN Layout & Spacing "Box height"; mock `.ss`, `.m .ss-t`). Not a strict loss of content, but it fails the purpose of reflow. *Fix:* below 360px wide, or when the text area is narrower than about 15 characters, drop the decorative magnifier and move the clear and submit buttons onto a row under the text area. Let the box grow to four lines before it scrolls inside itself. State it in DESIGN Layout & Spacing "Search results, phone".

- **[medium]** Focus after removing a Criteria chip is unspecified across the server round-trip (2.4.3, 3.2.2). Removal "pushes a new URL … and re-runs the search", then focus moves to the next chip (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Criteria chip"). If the chip row re-renders when the new results arrive, the focused chip unmounts and focus falls to `<body>`. Sending focus to the Smart search box when the last chip goes moves it backwards past the Search bar, away from the results that just changed. *Fix:* key chips by criterion so the survivors keep their DOM nodes, and move focus synchronously at click time (optimistic removal), not after the navigation settles. When no chips are left, move focus to the result-count headline instead of the box, which also reads the new count without needing the live region. Set `aria-busy="true"` on the results list while its skeleton cards show.

- **[low]** The focus ring rules for the box need implementation detail, or the ring will double up or be clipped (2.4.7, 1.4.11). The whole-box Gold ring "while the text area has focus" (DESIGN Components "Smart search box") needs `:has(textarea:focus-visible)`, not `:focus-within`, which would also ring the box while the clear or submit button is focused and draw two rings. The icon buttons sit 4px from the inner edge of the border (`.ss{padding:4px…}`), so their 2px-offset, 2px-wide ring reaches the gradient exactly. If `.ss` ever gets `overflow: hidden` for the two-line scroll, the ring is clipped, and where the Midnight gap is lost Gold meets Cyan at 1.17:1. *Fix:* use 6px padding around the icon buttons, never put `overflow: hidden` on the box (scroll the textarea itself), and replace the textarea's default outline only together with the box ring.

- **[low]** Forced-colours behaviour is described but not specified (1.4.11). In forced-colours mode, non-`url()` `background-image` is dropped, so the gradient goes. The box border is `transparent` under the gradient; Chromium usually paints transparent borders in the system colour, but that is not guaranteed across engines (DESIGN Colors "Forced colours"). The EXPERIENCE floor also calls the gradient "decorative", but it is the field's only visible boundary, so it is a 1.4.11 component boundary, which it passes. *Fix:* add `@media (forced-colors: active) { .smart-search-box { border-color: CanvasText; background: Canvas; } }` to the spine. Reword the floor to "the gradient is the field boundary and passes 3:1 along its length; the box is named by its label". Add a forced-colours Playwright emulation check.

- **[low]** The character counter's description and wording need care (1.3.1). The counter "appears from 250 characters" and is the field's `aria-describedby` (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Character counter"), so the target either does not exist before 250 or the reference dangles. "250 / 300" is read as "250 slash 300". *Fix:* keep the counter element always in the DOM (empty below 250) with a stable `id`. Give it a visually hidden reading, "250 of 300 characters used". Keep the one-off polite announcement at 300, and fire it also when a paste is truncated at 300.

- **[low]** The idle submit has no visual disabled state, and pressing it or Enter on an empty box does nothing silently (1.4.1, 3.3.1). In the mocks the `aria-disabled` submit looks the same as the enabled one (EXPERIENCE Smart search states "Idle"). *Fix:* render `aria-disabled` icon buttons with the root disabled treatment (an `on-surface-disabled` border, 5.04:1 on Midnight, and the icon in the same grey). On Enter or activation with an empty box, keep focus in the field and do nothing else, which is acceptable, but state it.

- **[low]** The weak-match heading is the right focus target, but its hint is not tied to it and there is no quick way back to the box (2.4.6, 2.4.3). Focus lands on "No strong matches for ‘ski chalet with a fireplace’" (EXPERIENCE Smart search states "Weak match"). "Try describing it another way." is a separate paragraph, and a keyboard user who wants to rephrase must Shift+Tab back through the Search bar. A screen-reader user also gets no count of the closest Hotels listed. *Fix:* add the hint to the heading's `aria-describedby`. Make the hint end with a link-styled button, "Edit your description", that moves focus into the box with the text selected. Optionally add "Showing the 12 closest hotels" to the hint.

- **[low]** The score breakdown table's semantics are underspecified (1.3.1). The spine says "a real table with a caption and header cells", and lists "Relevance (meaning, exact words)" as one row (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Score breakdown", Accessibility Floor). Two values in one row with one header is ambiguous, and twelve tables all captioned "Score breakdown" are indistinguishable in a screen reader's table list. *Fix:* one row per value, with `<th scope="row">` row headers ("Relevance, meaning", "Relevance, exact words", "Review score signal", "Price signal", "Ranking blend score") and a "Score" column header. Caption each as "Score breakdown, Jacaranda Garden Hotel". It must fit 320px without horizontal scrolling, otherwise the scroll container needs `tabindex="0"` and a name, which would contradict "outside the tab order".

- **[low]** The busy states around submitting are loosely specified (4.1.2, 2.3.3). The spinner swaps into the submit, and the Search bar is "read-only" for up to 2.5 s (EXPERIENCE Smart search states "Submitting"). The Search bar's fields are popover trigger buttons and its Search is a button, so "read-only" has no native meaning, and native `disabled` would drop focus if the guest is on one. *Fix:* the spinner is `aria-hidden`, and the submit keeps the name "Search by description" with `aria-disabled="true"`. Make the Search bar triggers and Search `aria-disabled` for that window, not `disabled`. The reduced-motion static spinner is fine because the status text carries the meaning.

## What passes

- Every new text and non-text pair, including the whole length of the gradient border (minimum 4.43:1 at the Brand Blue stop).
- A visible "Describe your stay" label, with the placeholder used only as an example.
- Two `role="search"` landmarks with distinct names, "Smart search" and "Search hotels", and the submit named "Search by description" so it is not confused with the Search bar's Search.
- Criteria chips as real buttons at a 44px minimum height, in a list labelled "Your filters", with a decorative ×.
- Notices that never take focus, at most two, in a stated priority.
- One polite "Match reasons added" announcement instead of one per card, and no reordering when reasons arrive.
- The skeleton pulse and the spinner both stop under reduced motion, and the gradient never animates.
- `enterkeyhint="search"` and the 16px phone input size, so iOS does not zoom on focus.
