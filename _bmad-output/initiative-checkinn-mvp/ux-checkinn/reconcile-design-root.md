## Changed on purpose (resolved by decision)

- **Audience:** the root's "projector only" became "projector + phones". Resolved by decision (.memlog Audience). The tie-break is stated: projector wins on desktop, phone wins below 768px.
- **Status colours:** the root's "No error, warning or success colour... add them deliberately" became Coral #FF6B6B and Mint #3DDC97, with no warning hue. Resolved by decision. Contrast was checked as the root asked.
- **Primary button labels:** the root's "Search, Book, Confirm" became "Search, Reserve, Confirm booking". This follows the PRD terms (FR-9, FR-18, FR-22). Resolved by decision (PRD is the product source).
- **Gold:** the root's "never a resting-state colour" was kept and extended to "never stars, warnings or badges", and Gold became the focus ring. Consistent with the root. Stars are white `[ASSUMPTION]`, logged.
