# Accessibility review: CheckInn UX spines

Reviewer stance: adversarial, WCAG 2.1 AA plus practical screen-reader, keyboard, touch, low-vision and projector use. Scope: `DESIGN.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`, with PRD §4 and §5 for context. Contrast ratios are computed with WCAG relative luminance from the token hex values.

## Verdict

Not ready to build against as written. Text contrast is strong everywhere: white 19.6:1, muted 15.8:1, Coral 7.1:1 and Mint 11.1:1 on Midnight, and white on Deep Blue 7.1:1. But the Night Blue / Deep Blue "state" surfaces fail non-text contrast (1.4.11) in the range calendar and make the disabled RESERVE button invisible. Several composite patterns also have AA-breaking gaps: the whole-card link with a button inside, the mobile sheets, the disabled steppers, and reflow with two fixed bars. Most fixes are token or one-line spec changes. The claim in EXPERIENCE "Accessibility Floor" that "all token pairs pass `npm run design:lint`" covers text pairs only. It must not be read as covering state indicators.

## Findings

- **[critical]** Range calendar states fail 1.4.11 and rely on colour alone. In-range days are Night Blue on Midnight (1.33:1), so they are effectively invisible, and on a projector certainly so. Check-in and check-out are Deep Blue on Midnight (2.76:1) and 2.07:1 against the adjacent in-range fill. The only cue for a guest's selected range is a fill difference that fails 3:1 (DESIGN Colors and `calendar-day-*`, Components "Range calendar"). *Fix:* give the endpoints a 2px Gold or white ring, or switch them to a fill of at least 3:1 against both neighbours. Draw the in-range band as a Brand Blue (4.43:1) outlined band or a continuous Brand Blue underline/track rather than a Night Blue fill. Add a non-colour cue such as an "In"/"Out" micro-label or a distinct shape (half-pill caps) on the endpoints. Separately, set `aria-selected` on the endpoints and give in-range days an accessible name that includes "in your stay".

- **[high]** The disabled RESERVE button vanishes. `button-primary-disabled` is Night Blue, and the `booking-summary` panel that holds it is also Night Blue, so there is no button shape at all. On the mobile bottom bar it sits at 1.33:1 against Midnight. A native `disabled` button is also removed from the tab order, so keyboard and screen-reader users never meet it or learn why it is unavailable ("Select at least one room." / party doesn't fit) (DESIGN `button-primary-disabled` and Components "Booking summary"; EXPERIENCE State Patterns "Nothing selected", "Party doesn't fit"). *Fix:* render disabled as a 2px dashed or solid `on-surface-disabled` outline on a transparent fill, so the shape survives on any surface. Use `aria-disabled="true"` (still focusable) with `aria-describedby` pointing at the reason text. On activation, move focus to or announce that reason instead of doing nothing.

- **[high]** The whole-card link contains a button, which is a nested interactive element. A `<button>` inside `<a>` is invalid HTML and gives two tab stops or an unpredictable single one. The link's accessible name also becomes the whole card text: name, stars, score, chips, prices, cancellation line, read as one blob (DESIGN Components "Listing card"; EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Listing card"). *Fix:* make the hotel name (`headline-sm`) the only link, with a stretched `::after` covering the card. Render "SEE AVAILABILITY" as a non-interactive `<span>` styled as a secondary button and marked `aria-hidden`, or as a second link with `tabindex="-1"` and `aria-hidden="true"`. Make the card an `<article>` and keep the rest of its content as plain text.

- **[high]** Mobile full-screen sheets and the desktop popovers have no dialog semantics. The spines say "popover" and "sheet" but never say `role="dialog"`/`aria-modal`, an accessible name, a focus trap, or that the background is inert. On mobile a full-screen sheet without a trap lets VoiceOver/TalkBack swipe into the hidden page behind it (EXPERIENCE Interaction Primitives "Popovers"; Responsive "< 768px"). *Fix:* below 768px, the calendar and occupancy sheets are modal dialogs: native `<dialog>.showModal()` or `aria-modal` plus `inert` on the rest, labelled "Choose dates" / "Guests and rooms", with initial focus on the check-in date or first stepper and focus returned to the field. On desktop, the popover is a non-modal dialog. The trigger field is a button with `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`.

- **[high]** "Opens on focus" for the Dates field breaks keyboard and screen-reader navigation and risks failing 3.2.1 on mobile. Tabbing through the search bar would pop the calendar open, and on a phone a full-screen sheet on focus is a change of context (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Range calendar"). *Fix:* open on click, tap, Enter, Space or Alt+Down only, never on focus.

- **[high]** Stepper and option buttons that become `disabled` at their limit drop keyboard focus. Pressing "+" until Adults reaches 10 disables the focused button, and focus falls to `<body>`, which in a sheet can also escape the trap. The same applies to the Rooms ≤ Adults coupling, where "−" Adults can silently disable "+" Rooms (DESIGN Components "Occupancy picker"; EXPERIENCE "Steppers announce…"). *Fix:* use `aria-disabled` rather than `disabled` on steppers and keep them focusable. Name them "Add adult" / "Remove adult" and so on. When Rooms is capped by Adults, announce the reason ("Rooms can't be more than adults").

- **[high]** Reflow (1.4.10) is scoped to the wrong level, and the fixed bars will fail it. The floor says "200% zoom", but 1.4.10 requires content at 320 CSS px wide, which is 400% at 1280px, and 1.4.4 requires 200% text. At 320 × 180 CSS px (1280 × 720 at 400%) a 64px header plus the fixed RESERVE bar leaves well under 100px of content. The desktop 100px header at 150% on 1280 × 720 (853 × 480 CSS px) takes about 21% of the height before the sticky summary. Seven 44px day circles do not fit a 320px viewport minus 30px of gutters (308px + gaps > 290px) (EXPERIENCE Accessibility Floor, Responsive; DESIGN Layout, `header.height`). *Fix:* state 400%/320px as the floor. Make the header static (non-fixed) below a viewport height of about 500px or at 320px width. Collapse the bottom bar to one 56px row and let it scroll away when the on-screen keyboard is up. Allow day cells to shrink to 40px with a 44px hit area through padding overlap below 360px. Set `scroll-padding-top`/`scroll-padding-bottom` equal to the fixed bars so focused elements are never hidden behind them, which also matters for the header RESERVE "scroll and focus".

- **[high]** Fixed heights will clip text under text-spacing overrides (1.4.12) and zoom. `button-primary` and secondary are 56px, `input` is 38px, `search-bar` is 72px, the header is 100px/64px, and `label-lg` has a fixed 30px line height with 1.5px tracking (DESIGN components). *Fix:* treat all of these as `min-height`, use unitless line heights, and let the search bar and buttons grow.

- **[medium]** The 38px input height contradicts the 44px touch-target rule in the same spine. Interactive chips (8px padding plus 14px text, about 38px), the header "Manage booking" `body-sm` link, the COPY button and the photo-viewer close are also unspecified (DESIGN `input`, `chip`, Do's "touch targets at least 44px"; EXPERIENCE floor). *Fix:* make input `min-height` 44px below 768px (48px is better for 16px text). State that every interactive element, including chips, header links, COPY, close and the photo prev/next, has a 44 × 44 hit area.

- **[medium]** Night Blue as a "raised surface" disappears on a projector and in daylight on phones. The booking-summary panel, toast, image placeholder and in-range days are all 1.33:1 against the page. Projectors crush near-black, so these become indistinguishable from Midnight for the room of 200 (DESIGN Colors "Primary subtle"). *Fix:* give every Night Blue surface a 1px Brand Blue border (3.32:1 against Night Blue, 4.43:1 against Midnight), as cards already have. Add the projector check, "every surface boundary visible at 1280 × 720 on a projector", to the EXPERIENCE Projector check.

- **[medium]** The Gold focus ring fails on light and photo surfaces. Against `input-light` (white) it is 1.61:1 and against Outline 1.08:1, and on imagery it is unpredictable. When a primary button is both hovered and focused, the Gold ring surrounds a Gold fill, so the focus indication is lost (DESIGN Colors "Tertiary", `input-light`; EXPERIENCE floor "Gold is the focus ring"). *Fix:* use a two-tone ring (2px Gold plus a 2px Midnight inner ring, i.e. `outline` plus `box-shadow`) on every element, and use a Midnight or Deep Blue ring on `input-light`. Or drop `input-light` from the MVP, since nothing in the spines places inputs on imagery.

- **[medium]** Mobile body text at 13px is too small for phone use at an expo. AA does not set a minimum size, but 13px Poppins at 1.56 in a dark theme, with muted metadata at 14px, is below comfortable reading. It also conflicts with the stated "large type" principle (DESIGN Typography "Below 768px … `body-md` to 13px"). *Fix:* keep `body-md` at 15–16px on mobile and reduce headings instead. Also, `body-lg` at weight 300 on a projector thins out badly. Use weight 400 at desktop widths, or keep 300 only at 24px and above with white rather than muted text.

- **[medium]** The two RESERVE buttons do different things. The header RESERVE scrolls to the table, and the summary RESERVE proceeds to step 2. Same name, different function, which works against 3.2.4 and confuses screen-reader users who list buttons. It also breaks "one primary per screen" (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns "Reserve (header)"). *Fix:* label the header one "SEE ROOMS" or "CHOOSE ROOMS" as a secondary button.

- **[medium]** Live-region and focus announcements will double up or race. Moving focus to the result-count headline and also announcing the count politely reads it twice. The Next.js App Router route announcer adds a third announcement, of the page title. Moving focus to the error summary and also firing an assertive live region does the same. Announcing the "Booking total" politely on every select change during rapid input is noisy (EXPERIENCE Interaction Primitives "Focus after navigation", Accessibility Floor live regions). *Fix:* use focus for navigation and the error summary, and the live region only for in-place updates (total, party warning, the room table refresh "6 room types available for 13–18 Nov"). Debounce the total by about 500ms. Render live regions empty on page load so they exist before content arrives. Set `aria-busy` on the results list and room table while their skeletons show, and announce "Loading rooms…" once.

- **[medium]** Focus is not specified for in-place updates and Back. When the availability picker refreshes the room table, the skeleton replaces the select the guest may have focused. Browser Back to Search results does not restore focus to the card the guest came from (EXPERIENCE "Availability picker", "Navigation"). *Fix:* after a table refresh, return focus to the availability picker field and announce the update. On Back, restore scroll position and focus the previously opened card's link, or the result headline if that is not possible.

- **[medium]** The search-bar validation pattern is incomplete. The message sits "under the field", but nothing says it is linked, and focus does not move. The Dates "field" is a popover trigger, so a date error has no input to associate with (EXPERIENCE "Invalid search input"; DESIGN "Input" invalid). *Fix:* on submit, focus the first invalid field. Link each message with `aria-describedby` and set `aria-invalid`. For Dates, attach the message to the trigger button. Apply the same `aria-describedby`/`aria-invalid` and `autocomplete="name"`/`"email"` (1.3.5) to the guest form, which the spines leave implicit.

- **[medium]** The toast has no timing or role, and it collides with the mobile bar. "Link copied" sits bottom-centre, and on the Hotel page on mobile that is exactly where the fixed RESERVE bar is (DESIGN Components "Toast"; EXPERIENCE "Share"). *Fix:* `role="status"`, visible for at least 5s and longer on hover or focus, positioned above the bottom bar, never taking focus. Or replace it with inline "Copied" text on the Share button.

- **[medium]** The confirmation reference and COPY button are under-specified. COPY has no accessible name beyond "COPY" and no stated feedback, and there is no clipboard-blocked fallback here (there is one for Share). `CI-7K3P9Q`-style codes read aloud to a room or typed from a projector are error-prone if the alphabet includes 0/O, 1/I/L or 5/S (EXPERIENCE "Booked", Flow 1 step 8, Flow 4). *Fix:* name the button "Copy booking reference". On success, change its label to "Copied" and announce that through `role="status"`. If the clipboard is blocked, select the reference text. Ask the PRD or architecture to generate references from an unambiguous alphabet, show them in a wide-tracked or tabular face, and give screen-reader users a spaced-out reading (for example, `aria-label="C I dash 7 K 3 P 9 Q"` on a non-interactive wrapper, or visually grouped characters).

- **[medium]** Room-table semantics are not defined for either layout. The quantity select needs a name that includes the room type, because "Rooms" alone repeats on every row. When rows become cards below 768px, the table header association is lost (DESIGN "Room table"; EXPERIENCE "Room table"). *Fix:* desktop uses a real `<table>` with `<th scope="col">`, and the room-name cell as `<th scope="row">`. Each select is labelled "Standard Double: rooms to book" using `aria-labelledby` with the row header. Mobile cards use a heading per room and visible labels for Sleeps, Price and Rooms. Sold-out rows keep a focusable or readable "Sold out for your dates" in place of the select.

- **[medium]** Price formatting reads badly in screen readers. "R 4 900" with a non-breaking space as the thousands separator is often read as "R four nine hundred", and "R 4 900,00" as "four, nine hundred comma zero zero" (EXPERIENCE Voice and Tone; DESIGN Typography). *Fix:* keep the visual format, but wrap amounts as `<span aria-hidden="true">R 4 900</span><span class="sr-only">4,900 rand</span>`, or verify with NVDA/VoiceOver at `lang="en-ZA"` and document the result. Set `lang="en-ZA"` on `<html>`.

- **[low]** The step indicator needs `aria-current="step"` on the current step and an ordered list. Its visual difference is weight plus the Cyan rule; muted (#E5E7E8) and white are near-identical on a projector (DESIGN "Step indicator"). *Fix:* add the semantics and prefix the steps with "1 · 2 · 3" numbers.

- **[low]** Disabled calendar days are distinguished from enabled days only by grey versus white text. Grey #7A828C is 5.04:1, readable, but the state is colour-only (DESIGN `calendar-day-disabled`). *Fix:* add a strikethrough or reduced-weight treatment. For days beyond the 30-night limit, use an accessible name such as "unavailable, stays are up to 30 nights", so the guest learns the reason rather than just "unavailable".

- **[low]** Calendar dismissal with a half-picked range is undefined. "Closes on DONE, Escape or outside click, keeping the picks" does not cover the case where only check-in is set. Outside click on mobile is impossible in a full-screen sheet (EXPERIENCE "Range calendar"). *Fix:* if only check-in is set, Escape or outside click reverts to the previous range. DONE is disabled through `aria-disabled` with the reason "Pick a check-out date". The "N nights" footer is a polite live region.

- **[low]** Photos, placeholders and the viewer: alt text is in PRD FR-10 but not in either spine. The image placeholder needs `role="img"` with the hotel's alt text, not just the visible name. The viewer needs dialog semantics, a focus trap, and a counter announced as "Photo 1 of 2" (EXPERIENCE "Photo viewer"; DESIGN "Image placeholder"). *Fix:* add these three lines to the Accessibility Floor. City tile photos are decorative (`alt=""`) because the tile's link name is the city plus "2 hotels".

- **[low]** Uppercase labels must come from CSS `text-transform`, not uppercase source text, or some screen readers spell out the words (SEARCH, RESERVE) (DESIGN Typography "Labels", EXPERIENCE button labels). *Fix:* say so in the spine, and author the strings in sentence case.

- **[low]** Smooth scrolling for the header RESERVE and the "Room ran out" return must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. The floor covers popovers and sheets only (EXPERIENCE "Motion"). *Fix:* extend the reduced-motion rule to all programmatic scrolling.

- **[low]** Not mentioned: a skip link, and landmarks. With a fixed header and a large search bar, a "Skip to results"/"Skip to main content" link and `<header>/<main>/<footer>/<nav>` landmarks are cheap (EXPERIENCE Information Architecture). *Fix:* add both to the floor. The logo link's name should be "CheckInn home".
